# Privacy

Berlin Airlift collects nothing.

- No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
- The app has no network permission and makes no network requests.
- Your recent cards and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
- NFC is used only while you are making or reading a card, and only to talk to that card.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
