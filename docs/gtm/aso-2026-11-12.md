# Store listing: Berlin Airlift (2026-11-12)

Goal from Taylor: owners searching for their Bonaverde Berlin, in whatever way they spell it, find this app, and the listing makes clear it is a community-made app that keeps their machines working. That is the whole message. The cautionary-tale story lives in the README and STORY, not the store.

## Where the brand can go

| Place | Indexed for search | Brand allowed? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| iOS app name | yes | Risky, with precedent: Apple approved a "for GoPro" name suffix after rejecting the brand in a subtitle | developer.apple.com/forums/thread/791158 (Jul 2025) |
| iOS subtitle | yes | No. That exact case was rejected under 2.3.7 | same thread |
| iOS keyword field | yes | No. 2.3.7: don't pack metadata with trademarked terms; Apple may edit keywords | developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ (read 2026-11-12) |
| iOS description, promo text | no | Fine as a plain compatibility statement plus disclaimer | 2.3.7 targets gaming search, not naming the hardware |
| Play title | yes | Avoid. Impersonation policy: don't imply a relationship | support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888374 |
| Play short description | yes, heavily | Moderate risk; OK with "community-made" in the same line | answer/9898842, answer/9888072 |
| Play full description | yes | Fine in natural sentences; no repetition lists | answer/9898842 |

Precedent: VerdeNFC (Bernd Trappe, App Store id1524099203) names "Bonaverde Berlin" only in its description, with no disclaimer, and is live.

Not checked: real search volume (autocomplete and the iTunes search API are blocked from this environment), and who owns the Bonaverde mark today (the company filed for insolvency in Oct 2019; a 2019 Berliner Zeitung piece says it moved to a licensing model).

## Search terms we want to catch

bonaverde, bona verde, bonaverde berlin, bonaverde app, bonaverde coffee machine, bonaverde roaster, bonaverde tags / cards / pouches, berlin coffee machine, roast grind brew, green coffee roaster, nfc coffee card. Covered by: iOS name (variant A), Play full description (all), Play short description (bonaverde berlin, coffee machine), keyword field (generic terms only).

**Google Play**
- Title (28): `Berlin Airlift: Coffee Cards`
- Short description (78): `Coffee card writer and reader for your Bonaverde Berlin. Free, community-made.`
- Short description, no brand (77): `Free coffee card writer and reader for the Berlin roast, grind, brew machine.`
- Full description: the block below (same text both stores).

Why: iOS does not index the description, so without the brand in the name, iOS searches for "bonaverde" never find us, and a rejection there costs one resubmit. Play indexes the full description, so the brand can stay out of the title, where a suspension would cost far more.

## Description (both stores, 1,655 chars, no em dashes)

```
Your Bonaverde Berlin still works. It just needs a card.

Berlin Airlift is a free, community-made app that writes the NFC cards the Bonaverde Berlin coffee machine reads to roast, grind and brew. Same machine, same green beans (some owners write it Bona Verde), and a simple way to keep it going.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a drink: full pot, half pot, overnight, roast only, grind and brew, or brew only.
• Hold a blank card to your phone.
• Put the card in your Berlin and press start.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Make reusable cards for roast, grind and brew.
• Set a pause so your beans roast now and brew later.
• What's on this card? you already have and copy it.
• Make maintenance cards: descale, clean the grinder, reset the air filter count.
• No card handy? Try the demo first.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A Bonaverde Berlin roast, grind and brew coffee machine.
• Blank MIFARE Ultralight EV1 NFC cards or stickers.
• Your own green coffee beans.
• An iPhone or Android phone with NFC.

MADE BY OWNERS, FOR OWNERS
Thousands of Berlins went out to coffee lovers. Berlin Airlift is built by the community so every one of them can keep roasting, grinding and brewing, with beans from any grower you like.

FREE, PRIVATE, OPEN SOURCE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The code is open source, and the card format comes from the work of the VerdeNFC and hackaverde projects.

Berlin Airlift is an independent community project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Bonaverde or by the owners of the Bonaverde and Berlin trademarks. Bonaverde and Berlin are trademarks of their respective owners and are named here only to say which machine the app works with.
```
