# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith. This project exists because strangers helped each other for free.

- Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, here or about anyone in the wider Bonaverde story, including the company's former staff.
- Credit other people's work.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove anything that breaks this, and may block repeat offenders. Report problems by opening an issue marked "conduct", or privately through GitHub's report feature on the comment.
